The Provenance Iceberg

What does provenance make visible?

Above the surface, a claim can look simple. Below it, origin, transformation, context and consequence determine whether the current state can still be understood.

Surface
Visible claim
Recoverable structure
Provenance Continuity across state transitions

Value

What matters, is useful, meaningful, or considered worthwhile.

Provenance provides origin and transformation history.

It allows us to follow where value was recognised, what it attached to, how it moved, and whether it changed.

Choice

The selection among available possibilities.

Provenance provides the decision path.

It preserves context, available options, constraints, branch points, selection, and consequence.

Agency

Capacity to act intentionally.

Provenance provides attribution.

It allows us to follow who or what acted, under what conditions, and what followed.

Identity

The self/system boundary and accumulated lineage of who or what something is.

Provenance provides lineage.

Identity becomes more than a static claim; it has origin and accumulated state history.

Conceptual relationship

VALUE -> CHOICE -> AGENCY -> IDENTITY -> VALUE

This relationship opens the research space. It is not presented here as a proven result.
