What the model means

Jielekeze starts somewhere else: the individual.

Most systems see people through categories:

  • location
  • income
  • employment
  • programme eligibility
  • demographic group

It asks what becomes possible when a person's goals, constraints, choices, lived experience and changing circumstances remain visible as they interact with the systems around them.

Validation questions

When does it hold, and when does it not?

  • Does making a system more legible actually change the choices available to an individual?
  • If choice conditions change, does agency change?
  • Does agency become action?
  • Does that action contribute to movement toward a state the individual themselves values?
  • When does it not?
