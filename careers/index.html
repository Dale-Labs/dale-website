FIVE FELLOWSHIP PATHWAYS

JIELEKEZE FELLOW

The lens follows identity, individual value, available choices and agency as a person moves toward a desired state. Provenance keeps that movement visible over time.

SHARED VALUE EXCHANGE FELLOW

This lens watches a shared system define value through interaction, movement, transformation and replication. The value-bearing carrier remains deliberately unresolved.

APE-AKFC FELLOW

This lens connects product identity with personal identity, then follows choice as it travels through a value chain. Choice travels. The pattern opens a question about value, consumption, production and seasonal state.

APE-AMMI FELLOW

This lens follows identity and value as they move across contexts. The question is whether value tied to one identity can transfer to another, and what happens if it does.

DALE LABS INNOVATION FELLOW

The Innovation pathway compares what repeats, differs, contradicts, or cannot yet be compared across the four pathways.
Fellowships

The DALE Draft asks a question of value.

It breaks value into its different elements and observes them through provenance. By recreating specific elements within the provenance chain of value, we ask what happens when we recreate the human experience of building value — for ourselves and for our communities.

VALUECHOICEAGENCYIDENTITY

The biggest questions of this century often begin in the same place:

How do we fix our systems?

The DALE Draft asks that question too.

But then it asks:

What if we built new pathways?

Working with complexity instead of against it.

Letting audacity and humility coexist.

Whether the challenge is equality, poverty or climate change, the Draft means going back to the drawing board — one challenge at a time.
